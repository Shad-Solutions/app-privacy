Privacy Policy — Fairturn

Last updated 2026-08-16

The short version

Fairturn collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests, so there is nothing for us to receive even if we wanted it.

What the app stores, and where

Anything you enter — such as class lists and names — is stored on your own device. It is not sent to us, and we have no way to see it.

One honest caveat worth stating plainly: if you have your phone or tablet's own backup switched on (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Google's backup on Android), your device's operating system may include this app's data in that backup. That is a feature of your device, not of Fairturn, and it is controlled entirely by your own device settings. We never receive it.

Children

Fairturn is designed to be used by adults — a teacher or group organiser — and is not directed at children as users. Any names entered are typed by the adult using the app and never leave the device. There are no accounts, no profiles, and no behaviour tracking.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. If your device backup included the app's data, that copy is governed by your device backup settings — you can remove it there.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. The app only records whether a purchase was made, on your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data held about you. Because we hold none, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase — your data is on your device and under your control. You are welcome to contact us to confirm this.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears at this address with a new date. If a change ever means the app starts collecting something, that will be stated clearly here before it happens.
