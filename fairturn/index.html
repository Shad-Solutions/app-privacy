Privacy Policy — Fairturn

Last updated 2026-08-16

The short version

Fairturn collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app makes no network requests, so there is nothing for us to receive.

What the app stores, and where

Anything you type, such as class lists and names, is stored on your own device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

If your phone or tablet backs itself up (iCloud on iOS, Google on Android), that backup may include this app's data. Your device settings control that, not Fairturn, and we never receive a copy.

Children

Fairturn is for adults to use: a teacher, or whoever is running the group. It is not aimed at children. Any names in the app are typed by the adult using it and never leave the device. There are no accounts, no profiles, and no behaviour tracking.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. If your device backup included the app's data, you can remove that copy in your device backup settings.

Purchases

Apple or Google handle purchases. We never see your payment details. The app records only whether a purchase was made, on your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase. Your data sits on your device, under your control. You are welcome to contact us to confirm that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting something, this page will say so before that happens.
