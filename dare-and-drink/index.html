Privacy Policy — Party Roulette

Last updated 2026-08-20

The short version

Party Roulette shows adverts. The advertising provider collects a device advertising identifier and basic technical information to serve and measure ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any personalised advertising is shown. You can decline, and the app still works.

What the app stores, and where

Anything you type, such as player names and any cards you write yourself, is stored on your own device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

On Android, this app is excluded from cloud backup, so what you type is not copied to Google Drive. If you move to a new Android device using Google's device-to-device transfer, your data can come with it — that transfer happens directly between your devices, and we never receive a copy.

Children

Party Roulette is for adults. It is not directed to children, it is rated 18+, and it must not be used by anyone below that age. We do not knowingly collect anything from children. Advertising in this app is not treated as child-directed, which is why an advertising identifier is used at all.

There are no accounts and no profiles. Anything typed into the app is typed by the adult using it and never leaves the device.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device. Because the app is excluded from cloud backup, there is no copy held anywhere else to delete.

Purchases

Apple or Google handle purchases. We never see your payment details. The app records only whether a purchase was made, on your device.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure and portability over personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or erase. Your data sits on your device, under your control. You are welcome to contact us to confirm that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting something, this page will say so before that happens.
