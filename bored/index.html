Bored: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Bored is a collection of small games for Android (and, later, iPhone), made by Software Hardware and Application Design Solutions Limited (SHAD), Ireland (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: we don’t collect any information about you. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t send anything to us.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves a small file on your device with:

  • your best scores, daily-game results and streak;
  • your progress through the level books;
  • your settings, such as sound and vibration;
  • how many free runs you have used of each game;
  • which games you have unlocked, so the app works offline.

This information stays on your device. We can’t see it. It’s deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings › Apps › Bored › Storage › Clear storage).

If you have Android’s backup turned on, Android may include this file in your device backup to your own Google account. That backup is made and kept by Google under your account and its settings, and we have no access to it.

Purchases

You can buy single games or the Bored Pass inside the app. Google Play processes every purchase: Google takes the payment, holds your payment details and keeps the record of what you bought, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. When you buy or restore a purchase, Google Play tells the app on your phone which games you own, so it can unlock them. We don’t receive your name, email address or payment details. The app doesn’t send your purchases anywhere.

If you want a refund, ask Google Play: Google handles refunds for Play purchases.

Sharing your scores

When you tap Share, the app makes a picture of your result and some short text, saves the picture briefly in the app’s own cache, and opens your phone’s share menu. You choose where it goes (a message, a chat, an email…). It goes only where you send it, and never to us. The text includes a link to the app’s page on Google Play.

What we don’t do

  • No account or sign-up of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share any personal information, because we don’t have any.

The app has the standard Android internet permission, but it makes no internet connections of its own. Google Play Billing (part of Google Play on your device) handles everything to do with purchases. The app also asks to use vibration, for the little buzz when you play.

Children

Bored is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because the app collects nothing.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we’ll update this page and its date before that version of the app is released. If we ever start collecting any information, we’ll say exactly what and why here first.
